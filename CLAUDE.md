# Indivisible Tahoe Truckee

## What It Is
Local chapter of Indivisible, a national grassroots organization focused on protecting democracy and resisting authoritarian rule. Annie is co-leader of Indivisible Tahoe Truckee, based in Truckee, CA.

## National Organization
- Website: https://indivisible.org
- Use national Indivisible branding whenever possible (logo, colors, fonts)
- National brand color: `#274475` (deep navy blue)
- When national assets aren't available, use the chapter brand defined below

## Chapter Brand Voice
- **Friendly** -- approachable, community-first, not preachy
- **Smart** -- well-informed, credible, substantive
- **Action-oriented** -- every piece of content should encourage people to do something
- Plain language, not political jargon
- Never corporate or generic

## Visual Brand (when not using national assets)
- Primary: `#274475` (Indivisible navy)
- Accent: `#CC2229` (red -- consistent with national political brand)
- Neutral: white and light gray
- Typography: clean sans-serif (match national site feel)
